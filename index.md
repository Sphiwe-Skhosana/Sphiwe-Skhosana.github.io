I am a lecturer of Statistics in the Department of Statistics at the University of Pretoria. I hold a  Ph.D. degree in Statistics from the University of Pretoria (2024), supervised by Prof. Lijian Yang and Prof. David Todem.  Working at the interface of statistics, mathematics, and computer science, I am interested in developing cutting-edge statistical methods for solving issues related to data science and big data analytics. The methods I developed have a wide application in engineering, neuroimaging, environmental studies, and biomedical science.

My research develops finite mixture models for data in which the relationship between a response and its covariates differs across unobserved groups. I work on semi- and nonparametric mixtures of regressions, robust estimation with contaminated Gaussian components, varying-coefficient models and directional statistics, and I apply these methods to South African and African data, including energy poverty and environmental economics.
