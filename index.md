I am a lecturer of Statistics in the Department of Statistics at the University of Pretoria. I obtained my PhD degree in Statistics from the University of Pretoria (2024). My research is at the intersection of probabilistic modelling, computational statistics and machine learning. My main research focus is on developing flexible computational and methodological techniques, in particular nonparametric mixture models, for detecting latent or hidden structure in the data. The methods I develop have been applied in energy economics, environmental economics and disease modelling.
