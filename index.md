My research develops finite mixture models for data in which the relationship between a response and its covariates differs across unobserved groups. I work on semi- and nonparametric mixtures of regressions, robust estimation with contaminated Gaussian components, varying-coefficient models and directional statistics, and I apply these methods to South African and African data, including energy poverty and environmental economics.
